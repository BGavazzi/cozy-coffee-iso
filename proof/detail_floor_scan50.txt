Mean by shelf count (wall run + L run only, sign+2menu present in every
case): 0 shelves -> mean +0.0116 (n=5), 1 shelf -> mean +0.0369 (n=21),
matching the direction and rough magnitude of the original 60-seed
correlation in "The detail floor at 40 plans" (0 shelves +0.0154, 1 shelf
+0.0383) -- the causal signal (a shelf adds roughly 0.02-0.03) reproduces
cleanly at this larger, current sample.

The per-room noise inside a single dressing state (0.075-0.145) is 3-6x
that signal (0.02-0.03), which is why identical dressing states land on
both sides of the 0.0 floor: plan 10 (no shelf) fails at -0.011 while plan
22 (also no shelf, same sign+2menu) passes at +0.061, a 0.072 gap between
two rooms with the literal same back-wall content -- see
proof/detail_floor_plan10_fail.png and proof/detail_floor_plan22_pass.png,
both rendered at the shipped 480px target (build_plan.py --seed N, no
--focal-scan): plan 10 reads -0.013 at 480, plan 22 reads +0.037, so the
gap is not a 320-only artifact either.
